Art Keeper

Scan your child’s art and schoolwork, and keep it organized by child and school year, with the story behind it in your child’s own voice.

Coming soon to the App Store

For iPhone and iPad.

What it does

Keep the art. Keep the story.

  1. 1

    Scan it, clean it up

    Scan a piece and Art Keeper straightens it. Fresh paper smooths out the creases and evens out the light.

  2. 2

    Kept by child and school year

    Each child has their own collection, sorted by school year. Art and schoolwork, together or apart.

  3. 3

    Record their story

    Ask “Tell me about this one,” and record your child’s voice or a video with the piece.

  4. 4

    Share, print, export everything

    Share any piece full size, print it at home, or export every piece and story in a folder for each child and school year.
